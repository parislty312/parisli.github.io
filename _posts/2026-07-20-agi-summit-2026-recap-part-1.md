## Yuandong Tian (Recursive) x Holly Zheng (EnvisionX)

Caught a compelling discussion between Holly Zheng and Yuandong Tian, co-founder of Recursive Superintelligence.

- Yuandong Tian talked about using AI agents for GPU kernel optimization. Even though it wasn't his main field, his team's AI built kernels that actually beat the specialists. The big takeaway? It's not just about better benchmarks. It's about how these agents let you dive into complex, new fields without needing years of extra training. It's not about what you already know, it is more about what "you + AI" can do in the future.

- Forget the old playbook of "find a market, then build a product." In the frontier AI world, that order is flipped. Now, your ability to do groundbreaking research is what actually drives your commercial potential. Research isn't just a side project anymore. There are lots of unknown factors about the model's capability yet, and researchers are probably the group of people closest to the answers.

- *"Knowing how to work with others is part of being smart."*

- **Focus on alignment, not just capital:** Holly made a great point. Money is easy to find in Silicon Valley. Real alignment is hard to find. Think of investors like long-term partners. Money buys you compute power and time. But it can't buy shared vision or good judgment.

- AI agents are starting to handle more tasks, like planning and executing work. This makes real human connection more important than ever. AI can mimic language, but it can't copy actual life experience or the meaning behind what we do.

- Yuandong thinks language models still have a ton of untapped potential. He writes sci-fi when he's not working, and he believes that language has always been the best way humans share what really matters.
